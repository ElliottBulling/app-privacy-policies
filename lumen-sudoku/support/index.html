Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore a purchase?

If you've previously purchased a puzzle pack and it isn't showing as unlocked, go to the Puzzle Packs screen and tap the pack. On the purchase screen, tap "Restore Purchases" to recover your purchase.

My progress isn't saving

Puzzle progress is saved automatically to your device. Make sure you're not force-quitting the app mid-puzzle. If the issue persists, please contact us.

I was charged but the pack is still locked

Try restoring your purchases first. If that doesn't work, contact us at codsworthphd@gmail.com with your Apple ID receipt and we'll sort it out.
